# Tayyab Naveed

I build browser products that have to feel instant while something complicated is happening underneath. Senior full-stack engineer, seven years in.

For the past five years that has mostly been [Roll AI](https://roll.ai), where I work on a browser video editor: zoomable timelines, audio waveforms on canvas, custom HLS players, and the playback coordination that keeps audio and video in step. I know what an L cut is and why your player stalls, and I am not sure which of those I am prouder of.

Lately my work has turned towards agents. I contribute to an MCP server that lets agents drive editor functionality and generate motion graphics, and I built a shared workbench that gives engineers and agents the same plans and architecture maps across repositories, so nobody starts an investigation from nothing.

Alongside that I run engineering at **Metascalers**, a small agency platform with paying customers, where I talk to the customers, decide what to build, build it, deploy it and keep it running.

### What I like working on

- Interfaces over complicated systems. Editors, timelines, dashboards, anything where the hard part is what the user should *not* have to think about
- Performance, measured rather than guessed. Instrument the loading path first, then argue about it
- Making codebases legible to agents, which turns out to be mostly about context and specifications rather than models
